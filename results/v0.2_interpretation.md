# v0.2 pilot results

## Main observation

The six-genome pilot produces two major clusters that match the two current genera represented in the manifest:

- **Teseptimavirus**: T7 and phiA1122.
- **Teetrevirus**: T3, phiYeO3-12, phiSG-JL2 and vB_YenP_AP5.

This is an exploratory result for this small panel, not a taxonomic validation or a genus-classification benchmark.

## Pairwise signal

T7 and phiA1122 share 93.06% nucleotide identity across 36,643 comparable aligned bases. Their shared aligned sequence covers 97.57% of the shorter genome and 91.75% of the longer genome.

Within the Teetrevirus group, the closest pair in this pilot is phiYeO3-12 versus vB_YenP_AP5:

- 94.27% nucleotide identity;
- 99.50% coverage of the shorter genome;
- uncorrected DECIPHER distance: 0.0887.

T3 is the most divergent member of the Teetrevirus subset represented here. Its identity to the other three Teetrevirus genomes is approximately 84.44%–85.29%.

Between the two represented genera, pairwise identity is lower than the strongest within-group comparisons. For example, T7 versus T3 has 76.08% identity, whereas T7 versus phiYeO3-12 has 67.33% identity.

## Distance clustering

Average-linkage hierarchical clustering of the DECIPHER uncorrected distance matrix gives the following ordering:

1. T7 — Teseptimavirus
2. phiA1122 — Teseptimavirus
3. T3 — Teetrevirus
4. phiSG-JL2 — Teetrevirus
5. phiYeO3-12 — Teetrevirus
6. vB_YenP_AP5 — Teetrevirus

The heatmap therefore shows a clear block structure for the two genera represented in this pilot.

## Important methodological caveat

The DECIPHER distance used here is not simply `1 - identity`. The current workflow uses `DistanceMatrix(..., method = "overlap", includeTerminalGaps = FALSE, penalizeGapLetterMatches = TRUE, correction = NA)`, so gap-containing alignment columns influence the distance calculation.

For that reason, the project reports **identity, alignment coverage, and DECIPHER distance separately** instead of collapsing them into a single similarity score.

## Interpretation boundary

Six genomes from two genera are enough to demonstrate the workflow and produce a biologically coherent pilot pattern. They are not enough to establish taxonomic thresholds, benchmark classification accuracy, or generalize to the full diversity of Studiervirinae.

The next version should expand the panel and compare the alignment-derived signal with curated taxonomy before making stronger biological claims.
